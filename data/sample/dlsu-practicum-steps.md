# Starting Your Practicum / Internship at DLSU: Step-by-Step

> Sample document written for this demo. It paraphrases the general practicum flow from a DLSU Student Services Center (SSC) infographic. Always confirm details with your Department Practicum Coordinator (DPC), since requirements and links can change.

## Before you start: prerequisites and support

- **Prerequisite:** Undergraduate students must finish **SAS3000** before they can enroll in the internship.
- **Career guidance:** Students who want help choosing a path can book a session with an **SSC Career Development Counselor**.
- **Orientation:** Attending the orientation run by your **Department Practicum Coordinator (DPC)** is mandatory. Schedules are posted in the Help Desk Announcement (HDA).

## Step 1: Choose your practicum site

- Internship openings can be found on **LSLINKS**, DLSU's career portal (lslinks.dlsu.edu.ph).
- **Companies to avoid:** do not choose companies involved in **gambling, tobacco, liquor, or third-party headhunting**.
- **Internal option:** you may also intern inside a **DLSU office**. This option does **not** need an Institutional MOA, so you can skip straight to Step 3.

The company or organization that hosts you is called the **Host Training Establishment (HTE)**.

## Step 2: Check for an Institutional OJT-MOA (IOM)

- An **IOM (Institutional OJT Memorandum of Agreement)** is the agreement between DLSU and the host company. Check whether your chosen HTE already has a **valid IOM** with DLSU using the list provided by your department.
- **If there is no valid IOM:** tell your DPC right away. The DPC coordinates with the **SSC Internship Placement Coordinator (IPC)** to draft one.
- **Processing time:** a new IOM takes about **14 to 21 business days**, including legal review and notarization.
- **When to request it:** request the IOM **during the term before your internship** to avoid delays.

## Step 3: Submit your application

- You can now apply as an intern to your chosen HTE. Some HTEs let you apply **while the IOM is still being processed**.
- **Important:** you may **not** start your deployment or log any hours **without a valid IOM**. Doing so violates **CHED** policy.
- If the HTE asks for a copy of the IOM, request it through your **DPC**.

## Step 4: Sign the Student MOA and Internship Plan

- The **Student MOA** is an agreement between **you and the host company**. Reviewing and signing it is part of your professional training.
- The Student MOA and the **Internship Plan** are handled by the **student and the HTE**.
- These documents do **not** need approval from the SSC or the University Legal Counsel (OULC). Your **DPC signs only as a witness**.

## Step 5: Start your internship

- A good practicum helps clarify your career path. Students are encouraged to show the **Expected Lasallian Graduate Attributes (ELGAs)** and use the **Lasallian Reflection Framework (LRF)** when facing challenges at work.

## Voluntary internships

- If a host organization requires a MOA for a **voluntary** internship (one outside the official curriculum), **DLSU will not be a party** to that agreement.
- The University supports students' growth but **assumes no liability** for incidents during voluntary internships.
